**Final prompt (verbatim):**

> Create one clean presentation sheet containing four distinct, self-contained Wren UI vignettes
> for no balances/assets, no inventory/collectibles, no connected apps, and no pending requests.
> These are artwork concepts only, not an interface mockup. Arrange four evenly separated
> vignettes on one plain charcoal presentation background with generous breathing room and no card
> frames. Each vignette has one clear focal object and no more than three meaningful objects. Use
> the wren in at most two. Show a tidy empty dark asset tray with one unmarked tag; a simple empty
> display plinth or shallow specimen shelf with a closed field notebook; a quiet dark window/device
> form with its connection path gently ending and the attentive wren nearby; and a clean resting
> transaction-slip holder with no papers, lit by a soft distant lantern. Match the supplied Wren
> artwork's warm, cozy, mature restrained pixel art: crisp controlled pixel clusters, selective
> dithering, strong silhouettes, limited texture, modern product-illustration cleanliness, and a
> consistent apparent pixel density. Use dark dusk ambience with localized straw-gold light, cool
> charcoal shadow, controlled feather brown, muted moss, and an occasional mist-blue accent. Avoid
> muddy brown-green casts, text, logos, currency, coins, Ethereum symbols, game UI, inventory grids,
> dialogue boxes, faux-wood interface borders, literal farming mechanics, oversized or cute/chibi
> mascot treatment, bright daytime color, clutter, generic fintech vector art, and watermarks.

Style references:

**Final prompt (verbatim):**

> Create one clean presentation sheet of twelve original, isolated decorative symbols from the
> Wren visual world: a feather, short twig, sparse branch tip, tiny wren footprints, curved flight
> path, compact lantern, simple garden gate, small field satchel, two stepping stones, seed-head
> grass, crescent moon, and modest five-petal wildflower. These are optional brand accents for empty
> states, section decoration, and loading moments—not core functional icons and not game inventory
> items. Arrange them in a precise four-column by three-row grid on plain charcoal without visible
> cells, frames, labels, numbers, or text. Keep each symbol concise and recognizable at roughly 24
> to 32 pixels. Match the references' mature restrained pixel art, consistent upper-left light,
> controlled pixel clusters, limited charcoal/feather-brown/moss/straw-gold palette, and product-
> asset cleanliness. Avoid game inventory styling, square slots, dialogue borders, faux-wood UI,
> bright colors, glossy gradients, heavy cartoon outlines, cute faces, rustic signage, literal farm
> mechanics, clutter, financial symbols, and watermarks.
