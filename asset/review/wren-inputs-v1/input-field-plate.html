Text-field language

Four controlled levels of menu-field influence. All keep ordinary UI text in Wren’s primary face; addresses, RPC URLs, and other technical values use the mono face.

01
Quiet inset
Current tactile language, turned inward. Safest and most neutral.
theme cue · low
02
Chiseled well
Closest to a Minecraft menu field, reduced to a crisp two-pixel rim.
theme cue · medium
03
Stepped corners
A small aliased silhouette with a quiet interior instead of a chunky frame.
theme cue · medium-high
04
Sunken groove
Minimal boundary for dense settings and advanced technical forms.
theme cue · very low
