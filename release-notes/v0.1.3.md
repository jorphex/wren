# Wren 0.1.3

Wren 0.1.3 adds local wallet creation and contract tooling, and hardens the
transaction, browser, permission, and restart boundaries introduced in 0.1.2.

## New

- Create an encrypted local wallet from either a 12-word recovery phrase or a
  directly generated secp256k1 private key. Wren uses the operating system CSPRNG,
  requires a confirmed encryption password, reveals the secret once, verifies the
  backup, and removes abandoned or expired staged signers.
- Prepare contract deployments through Wren's ordinary simulation, review,
  signing, and one-shot broadcast path.
- Inspect and publish bounded Solidity/Vyper standard JSON or Foundry/Hardhat
  build-info for an existing contract or confirmed Wren deployment. Sourcify is
  primary; direct Etherscan V2 is an explicit, credential-protected fallback.

## Improved

- Native Send now has consistent asset, contact, search, chain-marker, token-icon,
  and balance presentation, clearer address/copy evidence, and more predictable
  close, retry, queue, submitted, replacement, and monitoring states.
- Transaction review stays attached to a submitted transaction instead of
  immediately dropping back to Requests, and ambiguous broadcasts remain visible
  for recovery without being sent twice.
- Connected Apps now distinguishes current account access from global app
  activity. Per-account revocation uses an explicit consequence review, reliable
  focus recovery, and transient status feedback.
- Network editing has clearer endpoint status, bounded failover presentation, and
  usable short-window layouts. Upgraded profiles repair only Wren's recognized
  internal Companion origin so authenticated network catalogs remain available.
- Earn loads progressively, preserves public product context during partial
  failures, and applies the wallet privacy setting to every account-derived
  balance, amount, position, workflow, and receipt value.
- Generated-wallet, account-access, deployment, verification, network, Send, and
  signing surfaces have stronger keyboard, screen-reader, focus, scaling, and
  short-height behavior.

## Reliability and safety

- Profile migrations are covered from an exact schema-68/0.1.2 fixture through
  the 0.1.3 schema, including encrypted signers, permissions, origins, chains,
  contacts, tokens, and activity.
- Generated secrets are staged and committed before admission. Transaction
  lifecycle reservations are durably admitted before broadcast, distinguish a
  pre-invocation crash from an unconfirmed submission, and recover without a
  second send.
- Revocation and local-history clearing acknowledge durable profile updates
  before claiming success; write failures are reported as session-only or unknown
  instead of overstating what survives restart.
- Source-verification publication fences bind the exact forwarded destination and
  request, resume status checks without replaying accepted submissions, and remain
  bounded and non-secret across restarts.
- Dapp chain switching can be approved before account access without silently
  widening account permission, while denial, revocation, expiry, and event order
  remain origin- and account-scoped.

Wren 0.1.3 pairs with Wren Companion 0.1.2. Companion 0.1.2 provides Chrome and
Firefox identity switching, BaseScan/Etherscan connection compatibility,
tab-status aggregation, and transient network-catalog recovery. The mutually
authenticated local protocol remains version 3.
