# Wren 0.1.6

Wren 0.1.6 restores account renaming, makes account unlocking easier to find,
and brings wallet subviews onto one shared header boundary.

## Account names

- Account settings once again includes a clear Rename account action.
- Rename and Remove now sit as separate buttons in one balanced row.
- Local names remain distinct from ENS names. When ENS display is enabled, the
  interface can show both without changing the saved local name.
- Renaming also works for unnamed accounts, keeps drafts stable during background
  wallet refreshes, and provides reliable save, cancel, and keyboard focus behavior.

## Connected apps

- The global App activity view is now called Connected apps, matching what it
  contains.
- Its badge uses the same network, ownership, session, permission, and expiry
  rules as the destination, so stale or ineligible origins are not counted.
- Per-account Apps with access remains unchanged.

## Wallet navigation

- Requests, Balances, Activity, Account settings, and signing reviews now share
  the same 64px top bar and separator alignment.
- Selecting a locked software or hardware account from Accounts now continues to
  its password, device PIN, passphrase, or reconnect flow. Ready accounts still
  switch directly without an extra prompt.

## Activity

- Activity previews now follow their visible content instead of reserving four rows.

## Maintenance and compatibility

- Updated reviewed production and development dependencies. Coordinated major
  upgrades remain deferred.
- [Wren Companion 0.1.2](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/wren-companion/ifimccfajfbgligbhcgfapdagpnfkbhn)
  remains the paired browser extension over authenticated protocol 3. Firefox
  store review remains pending.

Linux x64 AppImage and deb packages remain Wren's qualified release target.
Windows remains an unsigned preview. macOS previews remain ad-hoc signed,
unnotarized, and unqualified until their exact artifacts pass physical Mac
qualification.
